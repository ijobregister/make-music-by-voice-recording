Android Native • Jetpack Compose • Kotlin 2.0 • Zero NDK

Turn your voice into any instrument.

VoiceSong Studio is a complete native Android Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) written in Kotlin. It captures human voice recordings—humming, whistling, singing, or beatboxing—extracts pitch contours and percussive transients in real time, and synthesizes authentic acoustic & electronic instruments: Erhu (二胡), Guzheng (古筝), Dizi (笛子), Grand Piano, Violin, Beatbox Drums, 808 Sub Bass, and Vocal Vocoders.

🚀 Quickstart Guide

VoiceSong Studio was designed so anyone without formal musical training can build full multi-track songs in under 2 minutes:

1

Pick or Create a Track

Select an instrument track (e.g. Beatbox Drum Kit, Erhu Lead, 808 Bass, or Guzheng Pluck). Choose the musical scale you wish your vocal pitch to snap to (e.g. Traditional Chinese Pentatonic Gong or Western Natural Minor).

2

Record Your Voice

Tap the Mic icon. Beatbox thuds and "ka" sounds for drums, or hum/sing melodic phrases for strings and synths. The live RMS waveform monitors your voice volume in real time.

3

Instant DSP Transformation & Preview

The app analyzes fundamental pitch and transient zero-crossing rate, synthesizes the chosen instrument, and plays an instant preview. Adjust octave shifts (-2 to +2) or switch instruments on the fly with a single tap.

4

Multi-Track Mixing & Master Export

Adjust Volume faders (0–120%), Pan (L100% to R100%), Solo (S), or Mute (M). Set your BPM tempo and loop length. When satisfied, tap Export to render an uncompressed 16-bit 44.1 kHz stereo .WAV master track and share it anywhere!

🛠️ Android Studio Setup & Build

VoiceSong Studio is 100% native Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. It requires no external native C++ toolchains or complicated NDK setups.

💡 System Prerequisites

• Android Studio Iguana, Koala, Ladybug, Meerkat, or newer.
• JDK 17 or higher (bundled with modern Android Studio JBR).
• Android device or emulator running Android 7.0+ (API Level 24+).

Clone & Open the Project

Clone the repository and open it directly in Android Studio:

Terminal (Git) 
git clone https://github.com/ijobregister/make-music-by-voice-recording.git
cd make-music-by-voice-recording
  1. Launch Android Studio.
  2. Click Open and choose the VoiceSongStudio directory (or cloned repo folder).
  3. Android Studio will automatically detect the Gradle Wrapper (`gradle-8.9`) and sync dependencies from gradle/libs.versions.toml.
  4. Connect your physical Android phone via USB/WiFi debugging, or start an Android Virtual Device (AVD).
  5. Click the green Run ▶ button (`Shift + F10`) to build and launch the app!

🎻 Instrument Catalog & Acoustic Principles

Every instrument in VoiceSong Studio is modeled using tailored digital signal processing algorithms that preserve human expression while imparting the acoustic physics of the target instrument.

Traditional Chinese Instruments (民族乐器)

🎻
Erhu (二胡)
Two-string bowed fiddle with a snakeskin-covered hexagonal resonator. Produces a haunting, nasal, and vocal-like timbre with continuous pitch glides and natural 5.2 Hz vibrato.
Stick-slip saw + dual python formants (1050 Hz & 3200 Hz)
🪕
Guzheng (古筝)
21-string Chinese plucked zither with movable bridges. Features a crisp attack, long shimmering metallic decay, and warm hollow paulownia wood body resonance.
Karplus-Strong physical pluck + 420 Hz body resonance
🪈
Dizi (笛子)
Chinese transverse bamboo flute with an extra hole covered by a thin reed membrane (dimo). Generates a bright, buzzing acoustic overtone and gentle breath air turbulence.
Dimo non-linear membrane saturation + 3.5 kHz breath bandpass
🎸
Pipa (琵琶)
Four-string Chinese pear-shaped lute played with rapid finger rolls (lunzhi). Known for its percussive, sharp transient plucks and rapid acoustic decay.
High-damping Karplus-Strong + 580 Hz body cavity filter

Western Instruments

🎹
Grand Piano
Concert grand piano featuring multi-harmonic additive synthesis with dynamic hammer strike velocity envelopes and 0.4 Hz unison string beating.
5-harmonic additive spectrum + unison beating
🎻
Violin / Strings
Orchestral bowed violin utilizing stick-slip sawtooth generation coupled with Hutchins violin body wood and air formants (280 Hz, 460 Hz, and 2800 Hz).
3-band Hutchins body filter + 5.8 Hz vibrato
🎸
Acoustic Guitar
Steel & nylon string acoustic guitar model utilizing low-pass filtered delay line feedback coupled with an 180 Hz Helmholtz air resonance filter.
Karplus-Strong feedback + 180 Hz body resonance
🎷
Saxophone
Warm reed wind instrument with non-linear reed pressure saturation and a 1400 Hz resonant brass bell formant filter.
Non-linear tanh waveshaper + 1.4 kHz horn bell filter

Beatbox Drums & Percussion

🥁
Beatbox Drum Kit
Analyzes chest thuds, vocal pops, and hiss sounds to trigger procedurally synthesized Kicks (160 Hz -> 48 Hz drop), Snares (180 Hz tone + 2.2 kHz noise crackle), and Hi-Hats.

🪇

Basslines & Vocal FX

🔊
808 Sub Bass
Tracks your vocal pitch down into the 35–130 Hz sub-bass register, applying warm soft-saturation overdrive to generate fat modern hip-hop sub hits.
Octave-shifted sub sine + 250 Hz low-pass saturation
⚡
Synth Funk Bass
Punchy, resonant sawtooth bassline with a 650 Hz resonant low-pass filter following pitch transitions.

🎤

Nearest-interval pitch snap + phase resampling

4-band IIR filterbank vocoder modulator

📐 DSP & Audio Architecture Deep Dive

VoiceSong Studio achieves ultra-low latency and deterministic audio synthesis entirely in Kotlin without requiring complex C++ NDK bindings. Here is how the processing pipeline functions:

A

AudioRecord Capture & RMS Gating

Captures 16-bit 44.1 kHz PCM audio on a dedicated Dispatchers.IO coroutine. Computes RMS energy across 1024-sample windows to dynamically isolate vocal signals from room silence.

B

YIN Fundamental Frequency (F0) Extraction

Implements the Cumulative Mean Normalized Difference Function (CMNDF) with a 0.15 aperiodic threshold and 3-point parabolic interpolation for sub-sample fundamental frequency estimation (65 Hz to 1200 Hz).

C

Transient & Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) Classifier

Computes spectral flux and zero-crossing density. High ZCR (>0.32) triggers Hi-Hats, low ZCR (<0.12) with fast energy rise triggers Kick drums, and mid ZCR (0.12–0.26) triggers Snare cracks.

D

Direct Form II Biquad Formant Filters & Sound Engines

Renders target instrument audio through dedicated mathematical generators (Karplus-Strong plucked feedback lines, stick-slip bowed friction saw waves, and additive harmonics).

E

Multi-Track Stereo Mixer with Soft Limiter

Applies constant-power stereo panning (cos/sin gain laws) and mixes tracks into interleaved stereo short arrays. A tanh soft-knee limiter prevents digital clipping distortion.

YIN Pitch Detection Implementation

Excerpt from com.example.voicesongstudio.dsp.PitchDetector.kt showing the difference function and Cumulative Mean Normalized Difference calculation:

app/src/main/java/com/example/voicesongstudio/dsp/PitchDetector.kt 
// Step 1: Calculate Difference Function: d(tau) = sum_j (x[j] - x[j+tau])^2
for (tau in 0 until halfWindow) {
    var diffSum = 0.0f
    for (j in 0 until halfWindow) {
        val diff = (audio[offset + j] - audio[offset + j + tau]) / 32768.0f
        diffSum += diff * diff
    }
    buffer[tau] = diffSum
}

// Step 2: Cumulative Mean Normalized Difference Function
buffer[0] = 1.0f
var runningSum = 0.0f
for (tau in 1 until halfWindow) {
    runningSum += buffer[tau]
    buffer[tau] = if (runningSum > 0f) {
        buffer[tau] * tau / runningSum
    } else {
        1.0f
    }
}

Beatbox Drum Transient Detection

Excerpt from com.example.voicesongstudio.dsp.TransientDetector.kt classifying beatboxing sounds into drum kit instruments:

app/src/main/java/com/example/voicesongstudio/dsp/TransientDetector.kt 
val drumType = when {
    // High zero crossing rate & high frequency energy -> Hi-hat ("tss", "chi")
    zcr > 0.32f -> {
        if (currentRms > 0.25f) DrumType.HIHAT_OPEN else DrumType.HIHAT_CLOSED
    }
    // Low zero-crossing rate and prominent low-end punch -> Kick drum ("boom", "buh")
    zcr < 0.12f -> DrumType.KICK
    // Mid-range burst with moderate zero crossings -> Snare drum ("ka", "pff")
    zcr in 0.12f..0.26f -> DrumType.SNARE
    else -> DrumType.TOM
}

Multi-Track Stereo Mixing Engine

Excerpt from com.example.voicesongstudio.audio.MultiTrackMixer.kt executing constant-power stereo panning and soft-knee saturation:

app/src/main/java/com/example/voicesongstudio/audio/MultiTrackMixer.kt 
// Constant-power panning law: cos(theta) Left, sin(theta) Right
val panAngle = (track.pan + 1.0f) * (PI.toFloat() / 4.0f) // 0 to PI/2
val leftGain = cos(panAngle) * track.volume
val rightGain = sin(panAngle) * track.volume

leftAccum += rawSample * leftGain
rightAccum += rawSample * rightGain

// Soft saturation limiter to prevent digital clipping
val limitedLeft = tanh(leftAccum)
val limitedRight = tanh(rightAccum)

stereoPcmChunk[frame * 2] = (limitedLeft * 32767.0f).toInt().coerceIn(-32768, 32767).toShort()
stereoPcmChunk[frame * 2 + 1] = (limitedRight * 32767.0f).toInt().coerceIn(-32768, 32767).toShort()

❓ Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: Why do I hear silence when recording my voice on an emulator?

Make sure your Android Virtual Device (AVD) settings have "Virtual microphone uses host audio input" enabled in Settings -> Microphone, or test on a physical Android device.

Q: How do I export my song and play it in VLC or send it to friends?

Tap the Export (Download) icon in the top app bar. Name your song and tap Render & Export WAV. You can then tap Share Master Audio to send the uncompressed 16-bit 44.1 kHz stereo audio file directly via WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, or Google Drive.

Q: Can I plug in external headphones or Bluetooth microphones?

Yes! Android's native AudioRecord and AudioTrack APIs automatically route audio through plugged-in USB-C audio interfaces, 3.5mm headphone jacks, and Bluetooth audio devices.
